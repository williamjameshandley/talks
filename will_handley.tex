\begin{frame}{An agent cannot do all the science in a paper}
    \framesubtitle{My rule of thumb: about 80\% of the work can be handed over}
    \begin{center}
        \begin{tikzpicture}
            \fill[C0!15] (0,0) rectangle (10.4,2);
            \fill[C1!25] (10.4,0) rectangle (13,2);
            \node at (5.2,1.35) {\Huge\textcolor{C0}{80\%}};
            \node at (5.2,0.55) {Work for agents};
            \node at (11.7,1.35) {\Huge\textcolor{C1}{20\%}};
            \node[align=center] at (11.7,0.5) {Requires\\a scientist};
        \end{tikzpicture}
    \end{center}
    \bigskip
    We decide what to calculate, and what to make of the answer.

    \medskip
    Those choices were human in every example you've just seen.

    \bigskip
    A paper produced by agents from start to finish leaves that part undone.
\end{frame}
